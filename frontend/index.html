Chatclub – free anonymous chat with random strangers

Chatclub pairs you with a random stranger for a private, text-only conversation. It is free, there is no signup or login, and nothing is saved: when the chat ends, the messages are gone.

How it works

  1. Pick a username and enter your age, country and gender.
  2. Optionally choose which countries and ages you want to meet.
  3. Press Start Chatting and get matched in seconds. Press Next to meet someone new.

Chatclub is for adults 18 and over. Be respectful and never share personal details like your phone number or address.
