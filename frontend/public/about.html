Free random chat with strangers

Chatclub is a free, anonymous text chat. Press one button and you're talking to a random stranger from somewhere in the world. No signup, no app to install, and nothing is saved.

How it works

  1. Create a quick profile. Pick a username and enter your age, country and gender. No email or password.
  2. Choose who you want to meet (optional). Pick one or more countries, or leave it on "Any country", and set an age range.
  3. Press Start Chatting. You're matched with someone who fits your filters, and whose filters you fit too.
  4. Chat, or press Next. Not a good match? Press Next to meet someone new. Press Stop to go back to the start.

Why people use Chatclub

  • Completely free: no subscriptions, no coins, no paywalls.
  • Anonymous: no account, no phone number, no email.
  • Private: messages are never stored. When the chat ends, it's gone.
  • Text only: no camera or microphone needed, so it works anywhere, even on slow connections.
  • Works on any device: phone, tablet or computer, right in your browser.
  • Country and age filters: meet people from the places and age group you're interested in.

Staying safe

  • Never share your phone number, address, school, workplace or social media with a stranger.
  • Never send money or gift cards to anyone you meet online.
  • If someone makes you uncomfortable, press Next or Report. You don't owe anyone a conversation.
  • Chatclub is for adults only (18+).

Frequently asked questions

Is Chatclub free?

Yes. Chatclub is completely free. There are no subscriptions, no payments and no premium features.

Do I need to sign up?

No. There is no account, email or password. You pick a username, age, country and gender and start chatting.

Are my messages saved?

No. Messages are passed directly to your chat partner and never stored in a database. When the chat ends, they are gone.

Who will I be matched with?

Men are matched with women and women with men. You can also choose which countries and which age range you want to meet, and matches only happen when both people fit each other's filters.

Why am I waiting for a long time?

Chatclub only matches you with someone who fits your filters. If nobody fits right now, try a wider age range or choose "Any country". The first visit can also take up to a minute while the server wakes up.

How old do I need to be?

You must be 18 or older to use Chatclub.

What if someone is rude or abusive?

Press Report to report them and move on to a new stranger. People who get reported by several users are disconnected automatically.
